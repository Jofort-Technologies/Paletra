The Product

A household journey, not a list of features.

Paletra connects money, food at home, meals, cooking and learning into one repeatable workflow.

01

Start With What You Have

Begin with your pantry. Paletra can identify meals from ingredients already available before suggesting new purchases.

02

Your budget. Your pantry. Your meals. One smarter food plan

Tell Paletra what you have, what you can spend, and who you're feeding. Paletra helps you decide what to cook, what to buy, and how to use what you already have.

03

Turn the Plan Into Meals

Meals are organized around your household, your budget and the ingredients you already have.

04

Let Juri Teach You

If you cannot prepare the recommended meal, Juri can guide you step by step and answer questions while you cook.

Juri Inside Paletra

The assistant that helps the household act on the plan.

Paletra is the product. Juri is the conversational intelligence users interact with.

Juri Can Recommend “Given your budget and pantry, these meals make the most sense this week.”
Juri Can Teach “Here is how to prepare the meal step by step. Ask me questions while you cook.”
Juri Can Learn “I noticed your household usually runs low on tomatoes mid-week.”
