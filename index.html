The Product

How Paletra Works

From what your household has to what it should buy, cook and learn.

01

Tell Paletra About Your Household

Set your budget, household size, preferences and what you already have at home.

02

Know What You Actually Need

Paletra combines your budget and pantry to help you decide what to buy—and what you can use first.

03

Get Your Food Plan

Turn your ingredients and budget into meals, a shopping list and a practical plan for the week.

04

Cook With Juri

Juri guides you through cooking, answers questions along the way and learns what works for your household.

Juri Inside Paletra

The assistant that helps the household act on the plan.

Paletra is the product. Juri is the conversational intelligence users interact with.

Juri Can Recommend “Given your budget and pantry, these meals make the most sense this week.”
Juri Can Teach “Here is how to prepare the meal step by step. Ask me questions while you cook.”
Juri Can Learn “I noticed your household usually runs low on tomatoes mid-week.”
